  <div class="container" >
    <p>The InnerSource Commons Foundation is seeking an experienced and highly collaborative Program Delivery Manager to lead community marketing, communications, engagement, research, and Summit promotion activities.

This individual will play a pivotal role in supporting the Foundation's mission by growing and activating the global InnerSource community, increasing participation in Foundation programs, promoting the annual InnerSource Summit, and generating actionable insights through community and adoption research.

This is a full-time, remote contract role (6-12 months, renewable based on organizational needs and funding), open to candidates worldwide. It reports to the Executive Director and/or the Foundation President/Chair. Compensation is commensurate with experience, location, hours, and contract structure.

To apply, please send us an email with a resume including examples of your work to info@innersourcecommons.org.</p>

  <h2>Responsibilities</h2>

- **Summit marketing and engagement:** Develop and run Summit marketing campaigns that drive awareness, speaker recruitment, attendee registration, and sponsor visibility, and produce post-event engagement and impact reports.
- **Community growth and development:** Design engagement programs, improve member onboarding, recruit and activate volunteers, grow participation in working groups and community calls, and track community health indicators.
- **Community experience research:** Run surveys, interviews, and feedback programs, map member journeys, and produce quarterly community experience reports.
- **InnerSource adoption and impact research support:** Support the market research consultant by gathering and organizing data and case studies, and turn findings into whitepapers, reports, and community resources.
- **Marketing, communications, and content:** Own the annual communications calendar, newsletters, blog posts, announcements, and brand consistency across channels.
- **Social media management:** Run day-to-day Foundation social media, build editorial calendars, and report monthly on performance.

  <h2>We're looking for someone with</h2>

- 5+ years of experience in community management, customer/client experience, marketing, communications, program management, or a related discipline.
- Demonstrated experience growing professional, technical, open source, developer, or member communities.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Experience developing social media and digital marketing campaigns.
- Experience managing virtual and in-person events, conferences, or community programs.
- Strong project and stakeholder management capabilities.
- Ability to work independently in a remote, volunteer-driven environment.
- Experience collecting, analyzing, and communicating qualitative and quantitative insights.

  <h2>Desirable Attributes</h2>

- Experience with InnerSource, open source, developer relations, or technical communities.
- Experience supporting nonprofit organizations, foundations, or professional associations.
- Familiarity with community health metrics, survey tools, analytics platforms, and CRM/community platforms.
- Experience developing research reports and thought leadership content.
- Knowledge of sponsorship, partnership, and community ecosystem development.

<h2>About the InnerSource Commons</h2>

The InnerSource Commons is a thriving community that empowers organizations and people worldwide to apply and gain the benefits of open collaboration in their internal work. The InnerSource Commons Foundation is a global nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing the practice of InnerSource by connecting practitioners, sharing knowledge, conducting research, and fostering community-driven learning through programs, events, and thought leadership.
