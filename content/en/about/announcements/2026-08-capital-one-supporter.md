InnerSource Commons, the world's largest community of InnerSource practitioners, today announced that Capital One has joined as a Supporter of the [InnerSource Commons Foundation](/about/sponsors/).

Capital One has been part of the InnerSource community for years, with its engineering leaders active in the InnerSource Commons FINOS Special Interest Group for financial services organizations, alongside peers from Deutsche Bank, Morgan Stanley and RBC. As a Supporter, Capital One now backs that work more formally, helping fund the community calls, patterns, and learning resources that practitioners across the industry draw on.

"We're glad to welcome Capital One as a Supporter of InnerSource Commons," said Russ Rutledge, Executive Director of InnerSource Commons. "They've shared how open collaboration inside a large organization changes the way teams build and share software, and their support helps carry that work forward for everyone."

More information about the InnerSource Commons Partner and Supporter programs, including the full list of Supporters, can be found on the [sponsors page](/about/sponsors/).
